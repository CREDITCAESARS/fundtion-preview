    What if my credit is bad?

    No worries — plenty of lenders never look at your score. They read your bank statements instead. That lane opens once your monthly deposits are steady.

    What if I don’t have a business?

    You don’t need one. The personal lane runs on your own file, and a sole proprietor can run a business round on an SSN — no LLC, no EIN.

    Will this hurt my credit?

    A little, short term — inquiries and new accounts, and both recover. Sequencing the applications is how we keep it to a minimum.

    Do you pull my credit?

    No. The check is self-reported and never touches your report. The only hard pulls are the ones you submit yourself.

    Do you need my social?

    Not for this. Nothing above asks for it. A lender needs one on the actual application — that is their form, and you are the one who fills it in.

    What does it cost?

    Personal and business funding rounds run on 0% introductory cards and no-doc business lines — sequenced by which bureau each bank pulls and what your file actually supports. The 0% is an introductory window, usually 6 to 18 months, set by the issuer and never by us.

    How a round runs

    From file to funded, in order

    File audit

    All three reports and the business file, read against what each lender actually underwrites — not against a generic score target.

    Position

    Bank the file

    Pre-application file review

    What actually gets a funding application declined

    Before a single application goes out we read all three reports and the business file against what the underwriter is going to see:

    • ✓ Utilization over 30%
    • ✓ Too many recent inquiries
    • ✓ Thin file, too few primaries
    • ✓ A low highest-limit ceiling
    • ✓ Young average account age
    • ✓ Recent lates or collections
    • ✓ Personal loan balances
    • ✓ Fraud alerts left switched on
    • ✓ Addresses that don't match
    • ✓ No real business foundation
