The score is a filter

Most people think approval works like this: score high enough, approved. It actually works like this: the score passes a minimum, then the lender reviews your whole file, then it decides.

MINIMUMS VS DECISIONS
A score gets you through the door. Each lender has a minimum for each product. Clear it and your application gets looked at. Miss it and it usually stops there. But clearing the minimum is not an approval. What happens next is underwriting, and underwriting looks at things your score doesn't show.

WHY TWO PEOPLE WITH THE SAME SCORE GET DIFFERENT ANSWERS
Picture two applicants with the same score.
The first has one card opened a few months ago, a small limit, no loan history, several recent inquiries and a new job.
The second has a file that is years old, several cards with real limits, a loan paid as agreed, no recent inquiries and the same job for years.
Same number, very different files. The second one usually gets the better answer.

WHAT YOU CAN CONTROL
Your payment history from today on.
Your card balances and when they report.
How often you apply.
How long you let new accounts season before the next application.
Whether the information on your report is accurate.

THE GOAL
The goal isn't just a higher score. It's an approvable profile. The next lessons cover the rest of what underwriting checks.
