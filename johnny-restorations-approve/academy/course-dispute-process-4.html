Send it and keep the paper trail

How you send a dispute decides how much proof you have later.

YOUR THREE OPTIONS
Online portals: fast and free, but you often click through a list of preset reasons, attachments can be limited, and some portals include terms you agree to. Save a screenshot of every screen you submit.
Mail: slower, but you control exactly what you say and attach. Certified mail with a return receipt gives you proof of what was sent and when it arrived.
Phone: useful for questions, weak as proof. If you call, follow up in writing.

Most people who want a strong paper trail use certified mail for anything that matters.

WHAT TO SAVE FOR EVERY DISPUTE
A copy of the letter and every attachment.
The certified mail receipt and tracking number.
The date it was delivered.
Any confirmation number or screenshot.
The response when it arrives, and the envelope it came in.

YOUR DISPUTE TRACKING SHEET
One row per account, per bureau, per round:
account name, bureau, what you said was wrong, date sent, tracking number, date delivered, response date, result, next step, next step date.

COUNT THE CLOCK
The investigation window generally starts when the bureau receives your dispute. Write the 30-day date on your sheet the day it is delivered.

WHILE YOU WAIT
Check your reports during the window. Bureaus sometimes update a file before the letter response arrives. Do not apply for new credit while a round is open.
