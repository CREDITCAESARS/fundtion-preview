Student loans

Student loans have their own rules and their own official help. Start with the official sources, not with a paid service.

WHERE TO FIND YOUR LOAN SERVICER
For federal student loans, log in at studentaid.gov to see every loan, its status and who services it. Private student loans are listed on your credit report and your statements.

LATE PAYMENTS ON STUDENT LOANS
Check each loan entry on all three bureaus. A student loan can show as several separate accounts, one per disbursement. Look for late payments reported for months you were in deferment, forbearance or an approved plan, or months you have proof of paying. Those are disputable errors with proof attached.

INCOME-DRIVEN REPAYMENT BASICS
Federal loans offer repayment plans based on your income and family size. If your payment is unaffordable, contact your servicer or use studentaid.gov before you miss payments. Plans and rules change, so always check the current version on the official site.

DEFAULTED FEDERAL LOANS
Federal loans in default have official ways back, such as loan rehabilitation or consolidation. They are explained on studentaid.gov. Read the details carefully, including what changes on your credit report and what doesn't.

USE STUDENTAID.GOV, NEVER A PAID "FORGIVENESS" SERVICE
You never have to pay a company to apply for federal repayment plans or forgiveness programs. Companies that charge upfront fees for this are a common scam. Never share your studentaid.gov login with anyone.
