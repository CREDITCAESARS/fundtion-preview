Group · 13 automatic messages

My Funding Offer

Everything a funding lead or client gets: booking, follow-up, approval and funded messages.

What changes when you approve

  • These 13 automatic messages switch on for Johnny Restorations.
  • They only send when another automatic message hands a contact over to it; or a lead or client reaches a step; or a contact's details change; or a deal moves to a new stage; or a call is booked or changed; or a form is filled in.
  • Until you approve, none of them can send.
  • Want one message different? Tap Request change on that message. The rest of the group is not affected.
