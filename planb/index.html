Where money slips through between a follower and a paid client, and the part of the system that closes each gap.

01
Now

Your bio’s call to action is “DM”.

The leak

Every lead waits on a person to reply. The longer they wait, the colder they get, and late-night DMs sit until morning.

The FUNDTION fix

The AI setter answers every DM in minutes, in your voice, asks the qualifying questions and books the call.

02
Now

Your link page has five buttons: a call, a free e-book, a checkout, a second e-book and a credit report offer.

The leak

Five choices with no order means many visitors pick none. One clear next step is easier to take than five.

The FUNDTION fix

One link in bio with a single first step, the scorecard. Every other offer shows up after it, matched to the lead.

03
Now

The free e-book button leads to a page that says “Page not found”.

The leak

A free offer is the easiest yes on the page. A broken link loses those leads silently, and nobody tells you.

The FUNDTION fix

We build and run every page in your funnel, and every link is checked before it goes live.

04
Now

Your bio leads with funding denials, but the booking page books a credit repair consultation.

The leak

A funding lead who clicks through lands on a credit repair call, which can feel like the wrong door.

The FUNDTION fix

A separate funding funnel and booking page, with the scorecard sending each lead to the right call.

05
Now

Your proof lives in your Results and Reviews highlights, and posts say “3 slots left”.

The leak

Proof that only profile visitors see does less work than proof in front of new people every day.

The FUNDTION fix

30, 60 or 90 reels a month that put your story and your offer in front of new viewers, with a community to keep them close.

Straight answers

Do I own the business?

Yes. Your brand, your clients, your payment account and your page stay in your name. FUNDTION builds the system behind it and runs it every day.

Why not just buy a snapshot or a course?

Because a template doesn't answer the phone. You'd still be the one setting it up, fixing it and sitting in every seat. We build it, run it, and upgrade it every month.

What does the build include?

Your whole company in your brand: lines, AI agents, funnels, calendars, pipelines, community, brand kit and your first 30 reels.

Is there a contract?

A 3-month minimum, then month to month. Everything is in a written partner agreement you sign before you ever see a payment link.

How fast is it running?

We start the day you're onboarded and aim to have you live within 24 hours. Phone carrier registration can take longer, and we show you where it stands. If you're not running in 14 days, your first month is free.

Do you guarantee clients or income?

No, and walk away from anyone who does. We guarantee the system is built and running. What you earn depends on your offer, your effort and your market.

Which plan should I pick?

Your scorecard results recommend one, based on the seats that are empty. Operators who already have a team usually hand us the expensive seats: disputes, ads, setters and content. We confirm it on the call.

I don't have a credit business yet.

Then this isn't your first step. Start with the Blueprint Academy, get your first paying clients, then come back and have it run.

Why this exists

I built two social pages past 130,000 followers each. Both got deleted. Each time, I rebuilt fast, using the same tactics, marketing and systems you see here. My current page hit 40,000 followers in 60 days.

Losing a page didn’t stop the business, because the business ran on systems, not on me sitting in every seat. FUNDTION is that same machine, run for you, in your brand.
