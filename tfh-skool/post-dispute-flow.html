Your dispute flow, round by round

Every round runs about 30 days, because that's the investigation window.

ROUND 1: clean up personal info, then dispute what's inaccurate, incomplete or unverifiable.
ROUND 2: read the results. 'Verified'? Ask how it was verified.
ROUNDS 3-4: go to the source: furnisher disputes and debt validation.
ROUND 5: escalate the lawful way: a factual CFPB complaint, or a consumer attorney if the law was broken.

The lessons are in Classroom > REPAIR & REMOVE. Post your questions under this post.
