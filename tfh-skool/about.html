Group rules · Proposed — added by FUNDTION

Her group has 3 rules, but their text isn't in the page data. These are proposed.

  1. Be respectful Everyone here is working on something personal. No judgment about where anyone starts.
  2. Protect your information Never post account numbers, your SSN, logins or full report screenshots. Blur before you share.
  3. Search before you ask Most questions are already answered in the Classroom or a pinned post.
  4. No self-promotion or DM pitching No selling, no links to other groups, no cold DMs offering services.
  5. No outcome promises Nobody here, admins included, can promise a score, a deletion or an approval. Posts that do get removed.
  6. Education, not legal advice For legal or tax questions about your own situation, talk to a licensed attorney or CPA.
