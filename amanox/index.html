What we found on @amanoxofficial.

Where money slips through between a follower and a paid client, and the part of the system that closes each gap.

01
Now

Your bio link opens on a card payment form for the full academy.

The leak

Asking for a card on the first screen skips the step where a visitor decides this is for them. Many leave before they read what is inside.

The FUNDTION fix

The scorecard comes first: two minutes, ten questions, then the booking page. Payment comes after the conversation.

02
Now

The booking link on your site is set up as an onboarding call for people who have already bought.

The leak

Prospects who want to talk before they buy have no clear call to book, so their questions go unanswered and the sale stalls.

The FUNDTION fix

A sales booking page backed by the AI booking line and confirmation calls, so people get answers and show up before they pay.

03
Now

One page sells business setup, credit, funding, Airbnb and real estate, branding and coaching.

The leak

When one offer covers everything, each buyer has to find their own reason to buy. A focused offer for one problem is easier to say yes to.

The FUNDTION fix

A credit funnel and a separate funding funnel, each with its own page and follow-up. The scorecard routes every lead to the one that fits.

04
Now

Your posts teach (like the 4 banks every new owner should know), and the next step from a post is the payment page.

The leak

People who learn from you but are not ready to pay today have nowhere to raise their hand, so follow-up never starts.

The FUNDTION fix

The AI setter replies to comments and DMs, sends the scorecard, and follow-up runs by text and call until they book.

05
Now

Contact is a phone number and an email address at the bottom of the checkout page.

The leak

A lead who calls after hours or emails on a weekend waits, and interest cools fast.

The FUNDTION fix

An AI customer service line that answers around the clock, plus the AI booking line that puts the call on your calendar.

From your public profile and links on October 15, 2026. Not an assessment of your revenue.

Straight answers

Do I own the business?

Yes. Your brand, your clients, your payment account and your page stay in your name. FUNDTION builds the system behind it and runs it every day.

Why not just buy a snapshot or a course?

Because a template doesn't answer the phone. You'd still be the one setting it up, fixing it and sitting in every seat. We build it, run it, and upgrade it every month.

What does the build include?

Your whole company in your brand: lines, AI agents, funnels, calendars, pipelines, community, brand kit and your first 30 reels.

Is there a contract?

A 3-month minimum, then month to month. Everything is in a written partner agreement you sign before you ever see a payment link.

How fast is it running?

We start the day you're onboarded and aim to have you live within 24 hours. Phone carrier registration can take longer, and we show you where it stands. If you're not running in 14 days, your first month is free.

Do you guarantee clients or income?

No, and walk away from anyone who does. We guarantee the system is built and running. What you earn depends on your offer, your effort and your market.

Which plan should I pick?

Your scorecard results recommend one, based on the seats that are empty. Operators who already have a team usually hand us the expensive seats: disputes, ads, setters and content. We confirm it on the call.

I don't have a credit business yet.

Then this isn't your first step. Start with the Blueprint Academy, get your first paying clients, then come back and have it run.
