What we found on @yunganurban.

Where money slips through between a follower and a paid client, and the part of the system that closes each gap.

01
Now

Your bio link opens straight onto a checkout for a paid 1-on-1 call.

The leak

A new follower is asked to pay before anyone has asked what they need. People who are curious but unsure tap away, and you never learn who they were.

The FUNDTION fix

A free 2-minute scorecard in your bio. Every visitor leaves a name and number, and the AI setter books the right ones onto your booking page.

02
Now

That checkout shows a different business name than your profile.

The leak

When the name changes at the moment of paying, buyers hesitate. Trust breaks right at the point of sale.

The FUNDTION fix

Every page, from link in bio to booking to payment, carries one brand: yours.

03
Now

Your bio sells four things at once: credit and funding, Airbnbs, car rentals and coaching.

The leak

A follower has to work out which one is for them. Several offers in one line split attention, and a confused buyer usually waits.

The FUNDTION fix

One front door: a credit funnel and a funding funnel up front. The scorecard shows what each lead needs, so the other offers come after, to the right people.

04
Now

Your captions say “book a call” and your free methods sit in highlights.

The leak

People take the value and leave nothing behind, so there is no way to follow up with everyone who watched and did not buy.

The FUNDTION fix

The AI setter answers comments and DMs in minutes, day and night, trades the free method for a number, and the AI voice lines follow up and confirm the call.

05
Now

You are speaking at a mastermind in Mansfield, Oct 16–17.

The leak

A room full of interested people with no single place to send them means most of those names walk out the door.

The FUNDTION fix

Your link in bio and scorecard live before you take the stage, so every person in the room can scan once and get followed up by the system.

Straight answers

Do I own the business?

Yes. Your brand, your clients, your payment account and your page stay in your name. FUNDTION builds the system behind it and runs it every day.

Why not just buy a snapshot or a course?

Because a template doesn't answer the phone. You'd still be the one setting it up, fixing it and sitting in every seat. We build it, run it, and upgrade it every month.

What does the build include?

Your whole company in your brand: lines, AI agents, funnels, calendars, pipelines, community, brand kit and your first 30 reels.

Is there a contract?

A 3-month minimum, then month to month. Everything is in a written partner agreement you sign before you ever see a payment link.

How fast is it running?

We start the day you're onboarded and aim to have you live within 24 hours. Phone carrier registration can take longer, and we show you where it stands. If you're not running in 14 days, your first month is credited.

Do you guarantee clients or income?

No, and walk away from anyone who does. We guarantee the system is built and running. What you earn depends on your offer, your effort and your market.

Which plan should I pick?

Your scorecard results recommend one, based on the seats that are empty. Operators who already have a team usually hand us the expensive seats: disputes, ads, setters and content. We confirm it on the call.

I don't have a credit business yet.

Then this isn't your first step. Start with the Blueprint Academy, get your first paying clients, then come back and have it run.
