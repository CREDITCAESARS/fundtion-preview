    Before you start

    Questions, answered honestly

    How much will my score go up?

    Nobody can tell you before they've read the file, and anyone who quotes you a number on a first call is guessing. It depends on what is actually on the report — how many items are inaccurate or unverifiable, how high the balances are, and how old the accounts are. Two people with the same score can have completely different reports and completely different results.

    Are deletions guaranteed?

    No, and no one can guarantee them. Accurate, verifiable information cannot be removed from a credit report, and anyone who tells you otherwise is selling you a story. What we control is what gets challenged, how it is challenged, with which bureau, and how hard we follow up when a bureau claims to have verified something it cannot show. What we don't control is what you do while we're working — running balances up, missing a payment, or taking new inquiries mid-round pulls directly against the file we are cleaning.

    What does it cost?

    It depends on what is on your report, and we tell you on the call — after we have read the file, not before. Before any work starts you get a written agreement setting out exactly what we do, what it costs and how long it is expected to take, together with your statement of rights under the Credit Repair Organizations Act and three business days to cancel at no cost.

    How long does this take?

    It depends entirely on the report. The bureaus have 30 days (45 in some cases) to answer each dispute round, and most files take more than one round, so think in months, not days. A report with a handful of inaccurate items moves fast; one with old collections across several bureaus and high balances takes longer. We'll tell you which one you are after we've read the file, not before.

    Can you remove things that are accurate?

    No. We challenge items that are inaccurate, incomplete, unverifiable, out of date or reported in a way the law does not allow, and we will not file a dispute that requires a false statement, for anyone. If an item is accurate and current, the honest answer is to pay it down, settle it, or let it age off — and we'll tell you which.

    Will disputing hurt my score?

    Disputing does not lower your score. What lowers it during the process is new hard inquiries, balances creeping up, and missed payments. Keep your utilisation low, pay everything on time, and don't apply for anything while we're working unless we've planned it together.

    What if I'm really after funding?

    Then the credit work is the first step, not a detour. Most bank and card programs start around 680, and applying underneath that mostly buys declines. Once the report is clean and the score is there, the funding side of the business opens up and we'll tell you when you're ready for it.

    What do you need from me?

    Government photo ID, proof of address dated within 60 days, and access to your full credit report — the confirmation email walks you through that. Everything we file has to be accurate — we will not claim an account isn't yours, or dispute a fact that is true, on any letter, for anyone.
