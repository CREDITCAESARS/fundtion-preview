Reading your results: deleted, updated, verified

Each bureau sends results for every item you disputed. There are three answers you will usually see.

DELETED
The item was removed from that bureau's file. Next steps:
Check the other two bureaus. A deletion at one bureau does not change the others.
Save the result letter. If the item ever comes back, this letter is your proof.

UPDATED
Something about the item changed. That can be good or bad. Compare the old and new versions line by line: balance, status, dates, payment history. If the update fixed the error you raised, mark it done. If it changed something else, or the error is still there, note exactly what is still wrong. That is your next letter.

VERIFIED (OR "VERIFIED AS ACCURATE")
The bureau says the company reporting the account confirmed the information. That is not always the end. Often the bureau sent an electronic code to the company and received an electronic confirmation back. The next lesson covers what you can ask for.

NO RESPONSE
If the deadline passes without a result, that has its own lesson in this round.

UPDATE YOUR SHEET
Log each result the day you get it, per bureau. Then decide the next step for each item: done, dispute the remaining error, ask how it was verified, or go to the company that reports it.

A REMINDER
Results differ from person to person. Some items come off, some get corrected, some are confirmed as accurate. Nobody can promise which.
