What 'fundable' means in real numbers

"Fundable" is a checklist, not a feeling. Use this one to score yourself honestly. These are common lender patterns, not fixed rules or promises.

PERSONAL SIDE
Scores comfortably above the lender's minimum on the bureaus they pull.
Several open accounts, at least some of them a year old or more.
A mix of card and loan history.
Card utilization low: under 30% at most, under 10% is better.
No recent late payments or new negatives.
No new accounts in the last several months.
Few recent inquiries.
A debt-to-income ratio the lender accepts.
Income you can document.

BUSINESS SIDE (FOR BUSINESS FUNDING)
Business formed and in good standing.
EIN, business bank account with real activity.
A business address and phone that are listed consistently.
An industry that isn't on the lender's restricted list.
A business credit file, with some vendor accounts reporting.
Business bank statements with consistent deposits.

YOUR READINESS CHECKLIST
Count how many of the items above you can honestly check off today. That count, not your score alone, is your real readiness. Most people starting out check only a few. That is normal.

THE HONEST TIMELINE
Going from a damaged file to fundable usually takes many months, and it differs for everyone. Anyone promising large approvals in days is selling something that doesn't work or isn't legal. Nobody can promise an approval or an amount.
