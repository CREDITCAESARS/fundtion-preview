Before you enroll

Questions, answered honestly

Can you guarantee items will be removed?

No — and anyone promising guaranteed deletions isn't being straight with you. Only inaccurate, incomplete or unverifiable information can be challenged. Accurate negative information generally stays. What we commit to is the work, the documentation, and that you'll always know where your file stands.

Are approvals guaranteed?

No. No credit repair company can legally promise you an approval, and any that does is one to walk away from. What we control is the work — we challenge what's on your file and hand you the exact plan to qualify. What we don't control is what you do while we're doing it. Opening new accounts mid-sweep, letting balances run up, or adding fresh negatives pulls directly against the file we're rebuilding, and it costs you approvals. The approvals shown on this page are from real clients who followed the plan, stayed consistent, and built credit while we repaired it.

What happens right after I enroll?

Your file handler reaches out within one business day for your documents. Once verified we pull your full reports, freeze the four secondary bureaus, and prepare round one. You get a message at every stage.

What do you need from me?

Government photo ID, Social Security card, and proof of address dated within 60 days. Nothing starts until those are verified — a legal requirement, not a formality.

What if I change my mind?

You have three business days after signing to cancel, with no cost and no obligation. Your written agreement spells out exactly what you paid for.
