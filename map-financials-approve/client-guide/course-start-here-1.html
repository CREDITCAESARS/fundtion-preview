Welcome: how your program works

Welcome to MAP Financials. This classroom is your guide while we work your credit file.

YOUR PROGRAM, IN ORDER
ONE: you sign your client agreement and send us what we need (next lesson).
TWO: we freeze the secondary bureaus and pull your reports.
THREE: we prepare and send round 1 to the bureaus.
FOUR: the bureaus respond, we tell you what came back, and the next round goes out.

WHAT YOUR PROGRAM INCLUDES
Every program (Basic, Boss and CEO Sweep) includes: all 3 major bureaus challenged, 4 secondary bureaus frozen with confirmations, 4 documented dispute rounds, updates every two weeks plus a direct line to us, and this guide and community.
Boss Sweep and CEO Sweep add the items listed in your written agreement.

WHAT WE CAN AND CAN'T DO
Only inaccurate, incomplete, outdated or unverifiable information can be disputed. Accurate information can't be removed, and nobody can promise a result, a score or a date. You also have the right to dispute inaccurate information with the credit bureaus yourself, at no cost.
