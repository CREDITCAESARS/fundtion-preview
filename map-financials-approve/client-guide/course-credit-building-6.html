Your 12-month rebuild calendar

WHAT YOU'LL LEARN
A general month-by-month shape for your first year. It's a guide, not a promise: your own file decides the details, so ask us when your situation differs.

WEEK ONE
Finish the three onboarding items: sign your client agreement, complete the secure intake form, and create your bureau logins. We freeze the four secondary bureaus included in your program. Read Read Your Credit Report so our updates make sense.

MONTHS 1 TO 4: YOUR DISPUTE ROUNDS
Your program includes 4 documented dispute rounds. Bureaus generally have 30 days to respond, and you get an update at every round and every two weeks.
Your part:
- Keep card balances low before each statement date.
- Set autopay on everything.
- Send us anything that arrives in the mail from a bureau or creditor.
- Don't apply for new credit or pay collections without asking us first.

AFTER YOUR ROUNDS
We review your file with you and talk through your next step. If your program includes tradelines, timing is part of that conversation.

MONTHS 5 TO 12: LET IT SEASON
This is the quiet stretch, and it matters. Accounts age, history builds.
- Open new accounts only when it fits your plan.
- Keep utilization low every month.
- Ask about soft-check limit increases where available.

THE HARDEST PART
Often the right move is to do nothing new and let accounts age. Opening something on impulse can reset that progress.

YOUR TAKEAWAY
Put your statement dates and a monthly "check my balances" reminder in your calendar today.
