Numbers cheat sheet

The numbers worth knowing, on one page. General information, not a promise about any file.

FICO SCORE WEIGHTS
Payment history 35%. Amounts owed / utilization 30%. Length of history 15%. New credit 10%. Credit mix 10%.

UTILIZATION TARGETS
Under 30%: damage stopped. Under 10%: good. A small balance on one card and zero on the rest: the common recommended setup.

FICO SCORE RANGES (300 TO 850)
800+ exceptional. 740-799 very good. 670-739 good. 580-669 fair. Under 580 poor.

HOW LONG ITEMS CAN REPORT
Hard inquiries: 2 years on the report, scored for 12 months.
Late payments: 7 years.
Collections and charge-offs: 7 years plus 180 days from the date of first delinquency.
Chapter 7 bankruptcy: up to 10 years from filing. Chapter 13: typically 7 years.
Closed accounts in good standing: often about 10 years.

DEBT-TO-INCOME (DTI)
Under 36%: comfortable. 36-43%: a common mortgage limit. Above that, lenders usually need other strengths.

DISPUTE TIMELINES
Bureau investigation: generally 30 days (up to 45 if you send more information during the investigation).
Re-inserted item: the bureau must notify you in writing within 5 business days.
Your right to cancel a credit repair contract: 3 business days.

RATE SHOPPING
Auto, mortgage and student loan inquiries within 14 to 45 days count as one. Credit card applications always count separately.
