Your rights in plain English

General information about your rights. It is not legal advice.

UNDER THE FAIR CREDIT REPORTING ACT, YOU HAVE THE RIGHT TO
- A free copy of your report from each bureau. annualcreditreport.com is the official free site.
- Dispute information that is inaccurate, incomplete or unverifiable.
- An investigation, generally within 30 days.
- Have information that can't be verified removed.
- Be told in writing within 5 business days if a removed item is put back.
- Know who has pulled your report.
- A free report after a lender takes adverse action against you.
- Take legal action for violations.

UNDER THE FAIR DEBT COLLECTION PRACTICES ACT, WITH DEBT COLLECTORS
- Request validation of a debt within 30 days of first contact.
- Ask them in writing to stop contacting you.
- Not be contacted before 8 a.m. or after 9 p.m.
- Not be contacted at work after you tell them not to.
- Not be harassed, threatened or lied to.

UNDER THE CREDIT REPAIR ORGANIZATIONS ACT, WITH ANY CREDIT REPAIR COMPANY, INCLUDING US
- A written contract before any work begins.
- A written statement of your rights.
- 3 business days to cancel for any reason.
- No promises of specific results or score increases.
- You can dispute inaccurate information with the bureaus yourself, at no cost.

IF YOU'RE A VICTIM OF IDENTITY THEFT
IdentityTheft.gov is the official Federal Trade Commission site. It creates a recovery plan and an identity theft report. Tell us right away, because it changes how we approach your file.

IF A COMPANY WON'T FIX AN ERROR
You can file a free complaint with the Consumer Financial Protection Bureau at consumerfinance.gov. We'll tell you if that makes sense for your file.

ONE HONEST NOTE
MAP Financials is not a law firm and doesn't give legal or tax advice. When your situation needs an attorney or a tax professional, we'll tell you.
