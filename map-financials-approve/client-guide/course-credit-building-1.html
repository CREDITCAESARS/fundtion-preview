Deletion is half the job

WHAT YOU'LL LEARN
Why a clean report isn't the same as a strong one, and what lenders look for.

Disputes deal with information that is inaccurate, incomplete, outdated or unverifiable. That's half of the picture. The other half is positive history: lenders look for the presence of good accounts, not just the absence of bad ones. A report with very few accounts left can be hard for a lender to evaluate.

WHAT LENDERS GENERALLY LIKE TO SEE
- Several open accounts in good standing.
- A mix of revolving (cards) and installment (loans) accounts.
- Some accounts that are 12 months old or more.
- Low card balances compared to limits, month after month.
- No new accounts in the few months before an application.
- No recent late payments.

Building that kind of profile takes time, usually a year or more, and you can start understanding it now while your rounds run.

ONE TIMING NOTE
Some of what follows involves opening accounts. While your dispute rounds are running, check with us before you apply for anything: we can see what's in progress on your file. The next lessons explain each product so you know what it is and can't be sold a bad version.

YOUR TAKEAWAY
Count the open accounts in good standing on your report today, and note whether you have both cards and loans.
