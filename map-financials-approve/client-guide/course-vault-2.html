Glossary

ADVERSE ACTION NOTICE. The letter a lender sends when it denies you, naming the reason and the bureau it used. Keep it and send it to us.
AVERAGE AGE OF ACCOUNTS. The total age of your accounts divided by the number of accounts.
AUTHORIZED USER. Someone added to another person's card. Not responsible for the debt, but the account can report on their file.
CHARGE-OFF. The creditor wrote the debt off as a loss. It can still be owed and still report. A paid charge-off is still a charge-off.
CPN ("CREDIT PRIVACY NUMBER"). Not a legal substitute for your Social Security number. Often a stolen number. Using one on an application is fraud.
CRA. Consumer reporting agency: the three major bureaus plus many secondary ones.
CROA. Credit Repair Organizations Act. The federal law for credit repair companies, including us: written contract, your rights in writing, 3 business days to cancel, no promised results.
DOFD. Date of first delinquency. Starts the 7-year reporting clock for most negative items. It does not restart when a debt is sold or paid.
DTI. Debt-to-income: monthly debt payments divided by gross monthly income.
FCRA. Fair Credit Reporting Act. Your right to accurate reporting and to dispute.
FDCPA. Fair Debt Collection Practices Act. Rules for debt collectors, including your right to request validation of a debt.
FURNISHER. Any company that reports information about you to a bureau.
METHOD OF VERIFICATION. Asking how a furnisher verified an item after a dispute.
PERSONAL GUARANTEE. Your promise to repay a business debt personally.
RE-AGING. Reporting a later first-delinquency date than the real one, keeping an item on the report too long.
RE-INSERTION. A deleted item coming back. The bureau must notify you in writing within 5 business days.
SECONDARY BUREAUS. Specialty reporting companies beyond the big three (for example for banking, rental or utility history). Your program freezes four of them for you.
TRADELINE. One account on your credit report.
UTILIZATION. Reported card balances divided by credit limits.
VANTAGESCORE. A scoring model created by the three bureaus, common in free credit apps. Most lenders use FICO scores.
