What we need from you

Nothing starts until these three things are in. Each one takes a few minutes, and the links are in your welcome email.

ONE, SIGN YOUR CLIENT AGREEMENT
It is on the intake form. Your written services agreement, the Consumer Credit File Rights disclosure and your 3-day right-to-cancel notice are linked there. Read them, ask about anything unclear, then sign. No work starts until they are signed.

TWO, COMPLETE THE SECURE INTAKE FORM
Your legal name, your addresses for the last two years, a photo ID, proof of address and your credit monitoring access.

THREE, CREATE YOUR BUREAU LOGINS
Free accounts at all 3 bureaus, so we see results the day they post. The bureau form walks you through it step by step.

You can cancel your contract without penalty within 3 business days.
