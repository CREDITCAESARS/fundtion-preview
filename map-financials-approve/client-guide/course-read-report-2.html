An account, field by field

WHAT YOU'LL LEARN
How to read one account (a tradeline) on your report, field by field.

CREDITOR NAME
Who reports the account. On a collection this is the collection agency, not the original creditor; there is a separate original-creditor field.

ACCOUNT NUMBER
Usually partly hidden. Two agencies reporting the same debt under different account numbers is a common sign of a debt reported twice.

ACCOUNT TYPE
Revolving (credit cards), installment (fixed payments with an end date), mortgage, or open (a charge card paid in full each month). Type matters for your credit mix.

ACCOUNT STATUS
Open, closed, paid, charge-off, collection, included in bankruptcy, repossession, settled for less than the full balance, or transferred. A paid charge-off is still a charge-off: paying it does not remove it.

RESPONSIBILITY
Individual, joint, authorized user, co-signer. If it says authorized user, you are not legally responsible for that debt.

CREDIT LIMIT AND HIGH BALANCE
On a credit card, the limit is what your utilization is measured against. A card reporting a balance with a zero or blank limit can be scored as maxed out. That is worth raising with us.

BALANCE
What the creditor last reported, usually your statement balance, not what you owe today.

PAST DUE
Any amount here is current and negative.

PAYMENT STATUS AND PAYMENT HISTORY
Current, 30, 60, 90, 120+ days late, or charged off. The month-by-month history grid shows each month's status. A late payment that shifts months, or a history that doesn't match the date of last payment, can be inaccurate reporting.

TERMS
The monthly payment and length. Lenders use the monthly payment to calculate your debt-to-income ratio.

REMARKS
Comments such as "closed by credit grantor", "settled for less than full balance" or "consumer disputes this account". Human underwriters read these.

DATES
There are several, and they get their own lesson next.

YOUR TAKEAWAY
Pick one account on your report and write out every field above for it. If a field doesn't make sense, ask in the Questions category (no account numbers) or email admin@mapfinancials.com.
