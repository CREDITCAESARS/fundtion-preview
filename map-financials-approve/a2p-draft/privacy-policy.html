Text messaging

Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

Service providers

We use trusted providers to host our systems, send email and messages and process payments. They may use your information only to provide those services to us and must keep it confidential. Text messaging opt-in data is never shared, as stated above.

Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical and physical safeguards. No method of transmission over the internet is completely secure.

Your choices

You can opt out of texts at any time by replying STOP, unsubscribe from emails using the link in any email, and ask us to access, correct or delete your information by contacting us.
