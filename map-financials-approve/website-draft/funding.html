A note from Kent

Good credit is not the same as fundable.

Most people with a 680+ score apply for funding the way they apply for anything else: one card at a time, whenever they need it. Every application leaves a hard inquiry, the next bank sees it, and the limits shrink. That is why a strong file still comes back with small approvals or a denial.

What applying blind costs you
  • A hard inquiry on every application, and the next bank sees it
  • Smaller limits from every bank that sees those inquiries
  • Denials that make the next 12 months harder

The banks are not random. Each one pulls a specific bureau, counts recent inquiries its own way and sizes your limit off the highest line you already have. Apply in the right order, inside one window, and the same file gets very different numbers.

That is the work. We read your whole file first, fix what an underwriter will flag, then line the applications up in the order your file can carry, with our funding partner running the lender side.
