An item came back (reinsertion)

Sometimes an item that was deleted shows up on your report again. That is called reinsertion, and the law sets rules for it.

THE WRITTEN NOTICE REQUIREMENT
A bureau may put a deleted item back only if the company reporting it certifies that the information is complete and accurate. If it does, the bureau must notify you in writing within 5 business days of reinserting it. The notice must tell you:
that the item was reinserted;
the business name and address of the company that certified it, and its phone number if reasonably available;
that you have the right to add a short statement to your file disputing it.

WHAT TO ASK FOR
If an item came back and you never got that notice, write to the bureau. Include a copy of the result letter that showed the deletion, the date you noticed it came back, and ask for the reinsertion notice and the certification the bureau relied on.

DOCUMENTING IT FOR ESCALATION
Put the deletion letter, the new report page and your letter in the folder. A reinsertion without the required notice is the kind of documented problem that belongs in a written complaint, and that you can show a consumer attorney.

PREVENTION
Keep every result letter forever. It is the only proof you have of what was removed and when.
