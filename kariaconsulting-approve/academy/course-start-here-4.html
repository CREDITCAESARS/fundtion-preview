PART 4: Find your stage (self-audit)

Take your 6 notes from PART 3 and be honest.

STAGE 1, FIX, if any are true: a late payment in the last 12 months; open collections or charge-offs; inaccurate negative items you haven't challenged yet.

STAGE 2, STRENGTHEN, if the file is clean but: card utilization is over 30% or a card is close to maxed; a pile of recent hard inquiries; accounts opened in the last 6 months; a thin file.

STAGE 3, FUND, if utilization is low (under 10% is the target), recent inquiries are few, and nothing is new.

STAGE 4, SCALE, if you've been funded and are building business credit.

THREE RULES FOR EVERY STAGE
RULE 1: Don't apply while you're learning. Every application is a hard inquiry.
RULE 2: Fix utilization before it reports. Balances report on the statement date, not the due date.
RULE 3: Apply in one planned window, in the right order.

Add your stage to your intro comment. Then: PART 5.
