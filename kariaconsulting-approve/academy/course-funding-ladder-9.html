Using capital responsibly

Getting approved is the start of the job. What you do with the money decides whether funding helps you or hurts you.

0% PERIODS END: PLAN FOR IT
Introductory rates have an end date. Write each date down, along with the balance and the rate that applies afterwards. Plan to pay the balance before the promotion ends, or know exactly what it will cost if you don't.

KEEP UTILIZATION IN MIND
High balances on cards that report to your personal file raise your utilization and can lower your score right when you need it. Know which accounts report where.

NEVER FAKE SALES TO GET CASH
Running fake transactions through your own business or a friend's to turn a credit line into cash, misstating revenue or income, or using a card in ways that break its terms can lead to closed accounts, clawbacks and legal trouble. It isn't a strategy.

TRACK EVERY DOLLAR
Keep a simple ledger: where each draw went, what it was for, what it earned or saved, and the payment schedule. Pay from the business account for business debt.

SET UP PAYMENTS
Automatic minimum payments on every account, plus a scheduled extra payment toward the balance you're clearing first. One missed payment can undo months of work.

WHEN TO GET HELP
If payments start to feel tight, contact the lender before you miss one. Lenders often have hardship options. Nonprofit credit counseling agencies can help too.
