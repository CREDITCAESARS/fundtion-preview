Structure your business foundation

Lenders run automated checks on a business before a person ever reads the file. A business that looks inconsistent can be declined before anyone reviews it. Most of this foundation is free or low cost.

FORMING AN LLC (OR OTHER ENTITY)
Form the entity with your state. An LLC is common for small businesses, but the right structure depends on your situation. Keep the filing in good standing with annual reports and fees.

EIN
Get a free Employer Identification Number from the IRS website. Never pay a third party for it.

BUSINESS ADDRESS AND PHONE
Use a real business address (many lenders don't accept a PO box) and a business phone number. List them the same way everywhere.

BUSINESS BANK ACCOUNT
Open a business checking account in the business name and run business income and expenses through it. Keep personal spending out of it.

ONLINE PRESENCE
A simple website and a business email on your own domain make the business easier to verify.

MATCHING DETAILS EVERYWHERE
Your legal business name, address, phone and EIN should match exactly on your state filing, IRS records, bank account, business listings and every application. Small mismatches cause verification problems.

INDUSTRY CODE
Many lenders restrict certain industries. Choose the code that truly describes what your business does. Never pick a code to hide what the business does.

TALK TO A CPA ABOUT TAXES
Entity type, how you pay yourself and what you write off all affect both your taxes and the income lenders see. Get professional tax advice before you decide.
