Personal loans

A personal loan is a fixed amount you repay in fixed monthly payments over a set term. It can be a useful tool, or an expensive one.

WHEN A PERSONAL LOAN CAN MAKE SENSE
Replacing higher-interest card balances with one fixed payment at a lower total cost.
A one-time expense with a clear repayment plan.
Adding installment history to a file that only has cards.

WHEN IT DOESN'T
When the total cost is higher than what you're replacing.
When you would run the cards back up after paying them off.
When the payment would push your DTI too high before a bigger goal like a mortgage.
When the only offers are very high cost.

FIXED PAYMENTS AND UTILIZATION
Moving card balances to an installment loan can lower your card utilization. That only helps if the cards stay paid down.

ORIGINATION FEES
Many personal loans charge an origination fee, often taken out of the loan before you receive it. Know how much you'll actually receive and what you'll repay.

COMPARE APR, NOT PAYMENT
A lower monthly payment over a longer term can cost much more in total. The APR includes interest and certain fees, so it's the better way to compare offers. Also compare the total repaid over the life of the loan.

PRE-QUALIFY FIRST
Many lenders show estimated rates with a soft pull. Compare several, then apply for one.

Nobody can promise an approval or a rate. Read every offer before you accept.
