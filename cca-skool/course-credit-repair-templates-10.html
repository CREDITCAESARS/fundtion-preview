📞 How to Dispute Inquiries Over the Phone

💥 Be confident. Be persistent. Don’t let them talk you out of your rights.

🔁 Here’s How It Works:

You’re going to call the credit bureaus directly and dispute unauthorized inquiries over the phone.💡 Yes — this works. You just need to be consistent, calm, and know what to say.

 

Step 2: The Script 📜

When you call, say something like this:

“Hi, I noticed a few inquiries on my credit report that I didn’t authorize these companies to run my credit — it looks like someone may have tried to apply for something in my name.”

Then make sure to include this part:

“According to the Fair Credit Reporting Act, im telling you these unverified and unauthorized inquiries must legally be removed.”

After that, list every inquiry or account that doesn’t belong to your open or closed accounts.

If the credit bureau tells you to contact the creditor directly, say:

“I already reached out and even filed a dispute,they told me they have nothing on file for me.”

⚠️ Important: Use their language. Don’t argue.
They may say they “can’t dispute inquiries” — but they can mark them as fraudulent, which is the same thing as them agreeing to remove.

✅ Always ask for a confirmation number before ending the call.

 

Step 1: Examples of Inquiries You can Remove Example: If you have a Citi Bank card that you opened in May 2018, and you have an inquiry from Citi Bank dated May 2019, this item cannot be deleted off.

Example 2. If you have an inquiry from Chase & you do not have an open or closed account associated with that account, you can get it removed.

  • ask the rep to transfer you to the fraud department. Bonus: If the rep gives you a hard time HANG UP! Get a new representative or ask ifa US representative is available
