The 3 things that move the business first

Chosen from the numbers above.

FIRST · 1

Know where every lead comes from

Only 18% of your new contacts in the last 30 days show a source. Without it we can't tell which posts, ads or pages bring the people who book and buy.

Measure: % of new leads with a source, every week.
FIRST · 2

Answer every lead and get more calls booked

About 5% of new contacts reach a booked call. 30% of lead messages waited more than 24 hours for a reply, and 90 follow-up tasks are open in your account.

Measure: lead to booked call, replies within 24 hours, overdue tasks.
FIRST · 3

Mark every call and see the money

Show rate, close rate and cash collected can't be measured yet. Calls aren't marked showed or no-show, and payments through Square don't show up in your account.

Measure: show rate, close rate and cash go from "not measured" to a real number.
