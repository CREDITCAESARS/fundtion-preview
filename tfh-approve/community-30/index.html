Day 4Myth or fact🛠️ Fix My Credit

Myth or Fact: Checking Your Own Credit Hurts Your Score 🤔

❌ MYTH: "I don't want to check my credit, it'll drop my score." ✅ FACT: Checking your OWN credit is a soft inquiry. Soft inquiries don't affect your score. What can affect it is a hard inquiry. That happens when you apply for credit and a lender pulls your report. So check your reports. Read them line by line. You can't fix what you won't look at. Drop a 🔍 if you've looked at all 3 bureaus this month.

Day 9Myth or fact🏗️ Build Your Profile

Myth or Fact: You Have to Carry a Balance to Build Credit 💳

❌ MYTH: "Leave a little balance on the card so it builds credit." ✅ FACT: You don't need to carry a balance or pay interest to build credit. Day 11Tip🏗️ Build Your Profile

The Statement Date Move Most People Miss 📅

Most people pay their card by the DUE date. That keeps you on time, which is the #1 thing. But here's what a lot of people don't know: the balance that usually gets reported is the one on your STATEMENT, and the statement closes before the due date. So if you want your utilization to show low: 1️⃣ Find your statement closing date in your card app 2️⃣ Pay the balance down a few days BEFORE that date 3️⃣ Still pay anything left by the due date Keep it under 30%. Under 10% is even better. Questions? Drop them below 👇🏾

Day 14Myth or fact🛠️ Fix My Credit

Myth or Fact: Paying a Collection Removes It 🧾

❌ MYTH: "Once I pay the collection, it comes off my report." ✅ FACT: Paying a collection doesn't automatically remove it. It usually updates to show it's paid with a zero balance, and it can keep reporting. Some newer scoring models ignore paid collections. Many lenders still use older ones. And if the collection is reporting something INACCURATE, paying it doesn't fix the inaccuracy. That's what disputes are for. Know what you're paying for before you pay. Questions below 👇🏾

If you're disputing, you need a log. A notebook or a note on your phone works. For every dispute, write down: 📅 The date you sent it 🏦 Which bureau 📌 The item you disputed 🔢 Your confirmation number 📬 The date you got a response and what it said The bureaus generally have 30 days to investigate. Day 19Myth or fact🏗️ Build Your Profile

Myth or Fact: Closing Old Cards Helps Your Score ✂️

❌ MYTH: "I paid it off, so I'm closing it. That'll help my credit." ✅ FACT: Closing a card can work against you. When you close it, you lose that card's limit. Your total available credit goes down, so the same balances on your other cards now make your utilization HIGHER. And over time you lose an open account with positive history. If there's no annual fee, keep it open and use it for something small every few months. Thinking about closing one? Ask here first 👇🏾

Set It and Never Miss: The Autopay Move ⏰

Payment history is the biggest part of your score. One missed payment can hurt more than people think. Here's the safety net: 1️⃣ Turn on autopay for the MINIMUM payment on every card and loan 2️⃣ Then pay the rest yourself, before the statement closes That way, if life gets busy, you're still never late. Day 29Myth or fact💳 Funding Prep

Myth or Fact: More Applications = More Approvals 📝

❌ MYTH: "I'll just apply everywhere. One of them has to say yes." ✅ FACT: Each application usually means a hard inquiry. A lot of inquiries in a short time can lower your score and makes lenders nervous. Hard inquiries can stay on your report for 2 years. Better plan: get your profile ready FIRST, then apply on purpose, not on impulse. Who's been through the "apply everywhere" phase? No shame, we've all been there 😅👇🏾
