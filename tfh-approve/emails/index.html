Preview · Emails

Your client emails

These are the emails your clients get automatically, from the moment they reach out to the day their file is done. We filled them in with a sample client named Jasmine so you can see exactly how they read. Nothing here has been sent.

1 · New leads

People who reached out but haven't booked yet

These go to people who filled out your form but haven't booked a call. They stop the moment someone books or buys.

Your free credit review is still open
When: The day after someone asks for a free credit review but hasn't booked a call yet
About what you told us
When: Four days in, if they still haven't booked and they told you why now is the time
Should I close your review?
When: Two weeks in, the last nudge if they still haven't booked or bought

2 · Booked a call

Someone booked a call with you

These confirm the call, help them get ready, and follow up kindly if they miss it.

You're booked. Here's what to have ready.
When: Right after someone books a call with you
Before our call: grab your report
When: Soon after booking (during daytime hours), asking them to pull their credit report before the call
I will call you in 2 hours
When: Two hours before the call
The call you almost took
When: If they miss the call or cancel and haven't rebooked yet

3 · New clients

Welcome and getting started

These go out after someone pays and becomes a client: welcome, agreement, and anything still missing from their file.

Welcome: the 3 things we need from you today
When: The same day someone signs up and pays to become a client
Did you sign your service agreement?
When: The next day, if they still haven't signed their agreement
One thing missing from your file
When: When something is still missing from their file (like an ID or proof of address)
Onboarding complete - what happens now
When: Once everything is in and their file is ready for work

4 · Client updates

While you work their file

Progress, alerts, wins, and a warm door back for anyone who stepped away.

Your latest results are in
When: When new results come back from the credit bureaus
A new late payment just hit your report
When: If a new late payment shows up on their report while you're watching it
Would you share your story?
When: After their first win, asking them to share their story
Your file is still here
When: About a month after a client cancels, an open door to come back
