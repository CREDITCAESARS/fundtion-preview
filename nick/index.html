01
Now

Booking a strategy session takes a paid deposit up front.

The leak

A lead who only wants to ask a question has to pay before they can talk to you. Many unsure buyers stop at the card form.

The FUNDTION fix

A free booking page with the AI booking line and confirmation calls and texts, so people book easily and show up.

02
Now

Your profile leads with business funding, and your site’s pricing page lists credit repair programs only.

The leak

A funding lead who goes looking for the next step finds credit repair plans, which can feel like the wrong door.

The FUNDTION fix

A funding funnel and a separate credit funnel, each with its own page and booking. The scorecard routes every lead to the one that fits.

03
Now

Your posts ask people to comment FUND, 800 or CREDIT.

The leak

Three keywords mean three kinds of lead, each waiting on a reply. The longer they wait, the colder they get.

The FUNDTION fix

The AI setter answers every comment and DM in minutes, in your voice, and sends each keyword down the right path.

Now

Your calls run Monday to Friday, 9 to 5.

The leak

People often scroll at night and on weekends. A lead who is ready on a Saturday has to wait until Monday, and interest cools fast.

The FUNDTION fix

An AI customer service line that answers around the clock, plus the AI booking line that puts the call on your calendar.

Straight answers

Do I own the business?

Yes. Your brand, your clients, your payment account and your page stay in your name. FUNDTION builds the system behind it and runs it every day.

Why not just buy a snapshot or a course?

Because a template doesn't answer the phone. You'd still be the one setting it up, fixing it and sitting in every seat. We build it, run it, and upgrade it every month.

What does the build include?

Your whole company in your brand: lines, AI agents, funnels, calendars, pipelines, community, brand kit and your first 30 reels.

Is there a contract?

A 3-month minimum, then month to month. Everything is in a written partner agreement you sign before you ever see a payment link.

How fast is it running?

We start the day you're onboarded and aim to have you live within 24 hours. Phone carrier registration can take longer, and we show you where it stands. If you're not running in 14 days, your first month is credited.

Do you guarantee clients or income?

No, and walk away from anyone who does. We guarantee the system is built and running. What you earn depends on your offer, your effort and your market.

Which plan should I pick?

Your scorecard results recommend one, based on the seats that are empty. Operators who already have a team usually hand us the expensive seats: disputes, ads, setters and content. We confirm it on the call.

I don't have a credit business yet.

Then this isn't your first step. Start with the Blueprint Academy, get your first paying clients, then come back and have it run.
