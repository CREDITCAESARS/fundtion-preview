What we found on @isaacfixmycredit.

Where money slips through between a follower and a paid client, and the part of the system that closes each gap.

01
Now

Your bio says to get started for a monthly fee, and the link opens a paid membership page with a Join button.

The leak

A credit repair buyer is asked to pay before anyone has looked at their situation or answered a question. Unsure buyers usually wait, and many never come back.

The FUNDTION fix

The scorecard comes first: two minutes, then the booking page. Payment comes after the conversation.

02
Now

Your title is Business Funding Specialist and your highlights carry FundingV1 and FundingV2, but the bio link sells credit repair, with funding only as bonus lessons.

The leak

A funding lead who taps through finds a credit repair membership, which can feel like the wrong door, so the funding interest goes nowhere.

The FUNDTION fix

A funding funnel and a separate credit funnel, each with its own page and booking. The scorecard routes every lead to the one that fits.

03
Now

Your membership page has no booking link and no phone number.

The leak

Someone with one question has no way to ask it before paying, and a small doubt is often enough to stop the sale.

The FUNDTION fix

A free booking page with the AI booking line, plus an AI customer service line that answers around the clock.

04
Now

Your profile has 36K followers and 7 posts, all luxury car carousels.

The leak

The posts show the lifestyle but not the service, so most followers never see what you do or why they should start now.

The FUNDTION fix

30, 60 or 90 reels a month about credit and funding, each ending with the scorecard, so the audience turns into booked calls.

05
Now

Your proof lives in highlights: Results 25, Repair 26, FundingV1 and FundingV2.

The leak

Proof that only profile visitors see does less work than proof placed where people decide to pay.

The FUNDTION fix

Your own reviews and score jumps on the pages where people buy, and the reels carrying them to new viewers.

Straight answers

Do I own the business?

Yes. Your brand, your clients, your payment account and your page stay in your name. FUNDTION builds the system behind it and runs it every day.

Why not just buy a snapshot or a course?

Because a template doesn't answer the phone. You'd still be the one setting it up, fixing it and sitting in every seat. We build it, run it, and upgrade it every month.

What does the build include?

Your whole company in your brand: lines, AI agents, funnels, calendars, pipelines, community, brand kit and your first 30 reels.

Is there a contract?

A 3-month minimum, then month to month. Everything is in a written partner agreement you sign before you ever see a payment link.

How fast is it running?

We start the day you're onboarded and aim to have you live within 24 hours. Phone carrier registration can take longer, and we show you where it stands. If you're not running in 14 days, your first month is free.

Do you guarantee clients or income?

No, and walk away from anyone who does. We guarantee the system is built and running. What you earn depends on your offer, your effort and your market.

Which plan should I pick?

Your scorecard results recommend one, based on the seats that are empty. Operators who already have a team usually hand us the expensive seats: disputes, ads, setters and content. We confirm it on the call.

I don't have a credit business yet.

Then this isn't your first step. Start with the Blueprint Academy, get your first paying clients, then come back and have it run.
