1 · Your day

Three check-ins. That's the job.

Open your app at the start of your day, after lunch and before you sign off. Each check-in, look at these places in this order.

Conversations

Left menu > Conversations

Every text, email and call in one inbox. Answer anything a real person asked that the bot didn't. Nobody should wait more than 24 hours.

Tasks

Left menu > Contacts > Tasks

Your to-do list. The system writes these for you: call this lead now, a client has a concern, add a new client to DisputeFox, a payment failed. Do today's, then mark them done.

Calendar

Left menu > Calendars

Your booked calls. Right now you're the only person on the booking calendars, so every call lands on you.

Opportunities

Left menu > Opportunities

Your boards. 01 Leads are new people. 02 Credit Repair Sales are people on their way to a call. 03 Credit Client Operations are your paying clients. 04 Funding are funding leads. Cards move on their own most of the time.

What the system handles

  • The first text to every new lead, the questions, and the booking link
  • Call confirmations and reminders
  • Follow-up emails for two weeks when someone doesn't book
  • Your AI phone assistants: Hope answers your main line (832) 415-3997 and Grace answers your client care line (216) 499-5248
  • Client check-ins, payment reminders and "results are in" messages
  • Marking no-shows at 10 AM and 3 PM, and starting the re-book follow-up

What you do

  • Run your sales calls
  • Clear your tasks every day
  • Reply to real questions within 24 hours
  • Post or schedule the reels we deliver
  • Record one reaction video a month (more is better)

2 · When a lead comes in

Minute by minute. On autopilot.

Here is what happens when someone fills out your credit survey on your credit page.

  1. Minute 0

    They answer your survey, tick the box that says you may call and text them, and land on your booking page with your open times.

  2. Minute 0

    They show up in Contacts, and a card appears on 02 Credit Repair Sales in the Qualified column. Their two-week follow-up emails start.

  3. Minute 1 Not on yet

    Hope, your AI assistant, calls them to help them book.

    Not on yet. AI calling needs a one-time setup in your account. FUNDTION is handling it.

  4. Minute 3 to 5

    If they haven't booked and it's 9 AM to 8 PM their time, you get a CALL NOW task. Call them inside 5 minutes.

  5. Minute 15

    Still not booked? The text assistant starts a short conversation and sends the booking link. If they go quiet, it follows up 3 times and then stops.

  6. They book

    The card moves to Call Booked. They get a confirmation and reminders. The bots stop texting them so they don't talk over you.

  7. Call day

    You run the call. If they don't show, the system marks it at 10 AM or 3 PM and starts the re-book follow-up.

Funding leads fill out your funding page and book a Funding Strategy Call. Their cards go on 04 Funding, and they get 4 emails over two weeks if they don't book.

Instagram and Facebook messages come into Conversations once your Page and Instagram are connected again. It's on your first-week checklist.

3 · When someone pays

From payment to Round 1.

Payment comes in

They're marked as a paid client and a card lands on 03 Credit Client Operations in the New Client column.

Agreement and intake

They get your intake link and your client agreement to sign. They also set up credit monitoring with MyFreeScoreNow ($1 to start).

Missing something?

If an item is missing, the client gets reminders and you get a task. When everything is in, onboarding is marked complete.

Bureau form

They get the form for the secondary bureaus. When it comes back, the card moves forward on its own.

DisputeFox Not on yet

You get a task: Add [client] to DisputeFox. Add them in DisputeFox, then mark the task done. After you send a round, move their card to that Round Sent column.

Once DisputeFox is linked (your invite in section 7), round updates flow into your app on their own.

4 · Your clients each month

Rounds, updates and reviews.

Rounds

  • Each client moves Round Sent > Waiting on Bureaus > next round on the 03 board
  • The bureaus have about 30 days to answer each round
  • When the file is done, the card moves to Complete and the client gets a graduation message

Updates

  • Weekly check-in texts to active clients
  • A day-30 check-in where the client rates you 1 to 10
  • When results come in, the client is told and you get a call task
  • A client reply that sounds like a concern becomes a task for you
  • A missed payment sends a reminder and pauses work. Work picks up again when they pay

Reviews

  • The best time to ask is the results call or graduation
  • Ask for a short video on their phone, in their own words
  • Never write a review for a client or promise a result to get one

1Your three daily check-insabout 75 seconds

Hey team. This is how we check the business every day. It takes three check-ins: morning, after lunch, and before we sign off.

First, Conversations. Every text, email and call lands here. If a real person asked something and the bot didn't answer, we answer. Nobody waits more than a day.

Second, Tasks. The system writes our to-do list for us. Call this lead. A client has a question. Add a new client to DisputeFox. We do today's tasks, then mark them done.

Third, the Calendar. Those are our calls for today. Show up early, and be ready.

Last, Opportunities. These boards show where every lead and client is. Most cards move on their own. If something happened on a call, move the card yourself.

Three check-ins a day. That's how nothing slips.

2What happens when a lead comes inabout 80 seconds

Let's walk through a new lead, minute by minute.

Minute zero: they fill out our survey and land on our booking page. A card shows up on the Credit Repair Sales board, and their follow-up emails start.

A few minutes later, if they haven't booked, you get a task that says CALL NOW. That one matters most. Call inside five minutes, while we're still on their mind.

At fifteen minutes, if they still haven't booked, our text assistant starts a short conversation and sends the booking link.

When they book, they get a confirmation and reminders, and the bots stop texting so they don't talk over us.

If they don't show up, the system marks it and starts the re-book follow-up. Your job is simple: answer the CALL NOW tasks fast, and show up for the calls.

3When someone paysabout 70 seconds

Somebody just paid. Here's what happens next.

They're marked as a client and land on the Client Operations board under New Client.

They get our intake link and our agreement to sign. They also set up credit monitoring for a dollar to start. If something is missing, they get reminders and we get a task.

Then you'll get a task to add them to DisputeFox. Add them, mark the task done, and get Round 1 ready.

After you send a round, move their card to that Round Sent column so everyone can see where they are.

Fast onboarding is the first thing a new client feels. Let's make it smooth.

4Taking care of clients every monthabout 80 seconds

Our clients hear from us every month. Here's how.

Rounds: each client goes from Round Sent, to Waiting on Bureaus, to the next round. The bureaus have about thirty days to answer each one.

Updates: the system sends weekly check-ins and a day-thirty check-in where they rate us one to ten. When results come in, the client is told, and you get a task to call them. Make that call. It's the best call of the month.

If a client replies with a worry, it turns into a task. Answer it the same day.

Reviews: on that results call, or at graduation, ask for a short video on their phone, in their own words. Never write a review for someone, and never promise a result to get one.

Steady updates are what keep clients with us.

5Reading your Friday numbersabout 65 seconds

Every Friday morning our KPI Scorecard updates on its own. Here's how to read it in fifteen minutes.

Open the sheet. Look at four numbers: leads, booked calls, shows, and sales. Then cash collected.

Compare this week with last week. Which number went down? That's where we look.

Lots of leads but few booked calls? We work on speed and follow-up. Booked but not showing? We work on reminders and confirming. Showing but not buying? We practice the call.

Pick one thing to fix next week. Just one. Tell the team on Monday.
