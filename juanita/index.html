01
Now

Your site’s “See Pricing & Get Started” buttons scroll to a booking calendar, and no prices are shown anywhere.

The leak

A visitor who taps for pricing and lands on a calendar has to decide to book without the answer they asked for, and some leave instead.

The FUNDTION fix

One clear first step, the scorecard, then the booking page, with every button saying exactly where it goes.

02
Now

Your bio leads with the funding you have secured, and the only call on your site is a credit repair consultation.

The leak

A funding lead who clicks through lands on a credit repair booking, which can feel like the wrong door.

The FUNDTION fix

A separate funding funnel and booking page, with the scorecard sending each lead to the right call.

03
Now

Your posts ask people to comment “FUNDING”.

The leak

Every comment is a lead waiting on a reply. The longer the wait, the colder they get, and late-night comments sit until morning.

The FUNDTION fix

The AI setter answers comments and DMs in minutes, in your voice, asks the qualifying questions and books the call.

04
Now

CAR APPROVALS is one of your three highlights, and your site does not mention cars.

The leak

A follower who came for a car approval clicks through and cannot find that path, so the offer that brought them in disappears.

The FUNDTION fix

Pages and reels built around the same offers, so every post points to a page that sells it.

05
Now

Your reviews live in your highlights and in a separate group page you send people to.

The leak

Sending a buyer off your site to read reviews pulls them away from the booking calendar at the moment they are deciding.

The FUNDTION fix

Your own reviews placed on the pages where people book and pay, and 30, 60 or 90 reels a month carrying them to new viewers.

Straight answers

Do I own the business?

Yes. Your brand, your clients, your payment account and your page stay in your name. FUNDTION builds the system behind it and runs it every day.

Why not just buy a snapshot or a course?

Because a template doesn't answer the phone. You'd still be the one setting it up, fixing it and sitting in every seat. We build it, run it, and upgrade it every month.

What does the build include?

Your whole company in your brand: lines, AI agents, funnels, calendars, pipelines, community, brand kit and your first 30 reels.

Is there a contract?

A 3-month minimum, then month to month. Everything is in a written partner agreement you sign before you ever see a payment link.

How fast is it running?

We start the day you're onboarded and aim to have you live within 24 hours. Phone carrier registration can take longer, and we show you where it stands. If you're not running in 14 days, your first month is free.

Do you guarantee clients or income?

No, and walk away from anyone who does. We guarantee the system is built and running. What you earn depends on your offer, your effort and your market.

Which plan should I pick?

Your scorecard results recommend one, based on the seats that are empty. Operators who already have a team usually hand us the expensive seats: disputes, ads, setters and content. We confirm it on the call.

I don't have a credit business yet.

Then this isn't your first step. Start with the Blueprint Academy, get your first paying clients, then come back and have it run.
