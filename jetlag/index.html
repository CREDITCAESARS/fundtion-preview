What we found on @jetlagrecoveryco.

Where money slips through between a follower and a paid client, and the part of the system that closes each gap.

01
Now

Your bio link opens a grid of five packages and a membership, each going straight to checkout.

The leak

With no quiz or call first, buyers have to diagnose themselves and pick a package. Unsure buyers often pick nothing.

The FUNDTION fix

The scorecard comes first and points each lead to the package that fits, before they ever see a checkout.

02
Now

Your home page and your bio-link page show different prices for the same package names.

The leak

A buyer who sees two prices for one package stops to wonder which is real, and doubt at checkout costs sales.

The FUNDTION fix

One set of pages and one set of offers, in one brand, kept in sync.

03
Now

There is no booking calendar or phone number on the path from your bio to checkout.

The leak

Buyers of a bigger package often want one conversation first. Without it, those sales wait or go somewhere else.

The FUNDTION fix

A booking page, the AI setter in your DMs and the AI booking line, so anyone who wants to talk first gets a call.

04
Now

Your posts sell primaries and tradelines, but your bio-link page does not list them as a product.

The leak

When posts and pages sell different things, a reader who clicks expecting what they saw cannot find it, and leaves.

The FUNDTION fix

Reels and pages built around the same offers, so every post points to a page that sells it.

05
Now

Your reviews live in highlights, and the pages where people pay show none.

The leak

Proof works hardest at the moment someone decides to pay. Missing there, it leaves the buyer alone with their doubt.

The FUNDTION fix

Your own reviews placed on the pages where people buy, and 30, 60 or 90 reels a month carrying them to new viewers.

From your public profile and links on October 15, 2026. Not an assessment of your revenue.

Straight answers

Do I own the business?

Yes. Your brand, your clients, your payment account and your page stay in your name. FUNDTION builds the system behind it and runs it every day.

Why not just buy a snapshot or a course?

Because a template doesn't answer the phone. You'd still be the one setting it up, fixing it and sitting in every seat. We build it, run it, and upgrade it every month.

What does the build include?

Your whole company in your brand: lines, AI agents, funnels, calendars, pipelines, community, brand kit and your first 30 reels.

Is there a contract?

A 3-month minimum, then month to month. Everything is in a written partner agreement you sign before you ever see a payment link.

How fast is it running?

We start the day you're onboarded and aim to have you live within 24 hours. Phone carrier registration can take longer, and we show you where it stands. If you're not running in 14 days, your first month is free.

Do you guarantee clients or income?

No, and walk away from anyone who does. We guarantee the system is built and running. What you earn depends on your offer, your effort and your market.

Which plan should I pick?

Your scorecard results recommend one, based on the seats that are empty. Operators who already have a team usually hand us the expensive seats: disputes, ads, setters and content. We confirm it on the call.

I don't have a credit business yet.

Then this isn't your first step. Start with the Blueprint Academy, get your first paying clients, then come back and have it run.
