Group · 5 automatic messages

My Payments

Messages about payments: failed or missed payments, recovered payments and refund reviews.

What changes when you approve

  • These 5 automatic messages switch on for ZZ FULLCHAIN E2E4.
  • They only send when a lead or client reaches a step; or something happens in your system; or a contact's details change.
  • Until you approve, none of them can send.
  • Want one message different? Tap Request change on that message. The rest of the group is not affected.
